Child safety standards

ontap: Beer & Drink Tracker, by Luca Drew Preston
Last updated: 18 October 2026

ontap has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to everything people post or send in ontap: photos, captions, comments, usernames and profile photos, on iPhone and Android.

Adults only

ontap is a drink-tracking app for adults. Everyone must confirm they are 18 or older (and of legal drinking age where they live) to create an account. We delete any account we learn belongs to someone under 18.

What's not allowed

  • Any content that sexualises, exploits or endangers children, including CSAM, whether real, drawn or AI-generated.
  • Grooming, or contacting or soliciting minors for any sexual purpose.
  • Sharing, requesting or linking to any such material.

How to report

  • In the app: on any post, tap the ⋮ menu, then Report, then Inappropriate or offensive. The post is hidden for you immediately and sent to us for review. You can also Block the person.
  • By email: luca@getontap.net with the subject "Child safety report". Include the username and, if you can, when it was posted. Do not attach or forward the material itself.

What we do

  • We review child safety reports as a priority, within 24 hours.
  • We remove the content and permanently ban the accounts involved.
  • We report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the relevant law enforcement and national authorities, as the law requires, and we preserve what they need.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement requests related to child safety.

Child safety contact

Our designated contact for child safety and CSAM prevention is Luca Drew Preston, luca@getontap.net.

See also our Privacy Policy.
